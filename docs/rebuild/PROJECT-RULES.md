# Nota Print project rules (durable)

Saved 13 Oct 2026 at Jake's direction. These rules apply to every existing and new asset, page and change on this project. If a rule conflicts with speed or polish, the rule wins.

## Authenticity
- Use the real official logo, as a source vector or the original source pixels, or a faithful trace of it. Preserve its exact lettering, shapes and colours.
- Never substitute a similar font for brand lettering, never invent a sign or mark, and never redraw third-party labels or logos.
- Audit assets that already exist against the original at full size. Reject any drift and replace it with the protected original pixels or a clean original. A convincing AI result is not proof that it is faithful.
- Feature the genuine logo confidently, with believable materials, depth or lighting, **without changing its geometry**.
- Interface fonts are editorial choices for the UI. They must never stand in for the brand's own lettering.

## Imagery
- Use the strongest real references at their best resolution. Crop, correct perspective, clean up and relight carefully, but never invent unseen details.
- For reference-led edits, prefer OpenArt Nano Banana Pro. Record the model, source, job IDs, output links and credits, **including failed jobs and retries**. A better model alone does not guarantee accuracy.
- Keep scale, camera and lighting consistent, and supply transparent cutouts where requested. When a reference is missing, the item stays **pending**. Never fill a gap with an invented item.
- No generative lettering for official logos.

## Design and delivery
- Premium, brand-specific, full-width imagery with concise type. Compact sections on mobile and useful interactions.
- No generic card clutter, no tooltips, no AI or "preview" captions on imagery.
- Test real forward and reverse scrolling, and reduced motion.
- Use existing connectors within the unchanged project credit caps. No top-ups, purchases or switching to API credits. Paid jobs need a **verified existing Nota allowance**; never infer one from another project's budget.
- Stay within this branch and the authorised Vercel **testing** (preview) project. No customer DNS changes, no live payments or supplier orders, and never claim that an unconfigured form sends mail.

## Nota Print specifics
- Keep the real logo-upload flow and deterministic, browser-side product rendering. Product geometry, print areas, colours and materials are reusable, and there is **no AI call per customer or per rotation**.
- Never present a flat image as a true 3D asset. Keep the product renderer lazy-loaded and the catalogue consistent.
- Suppliers are Laltex, Printful and Printify, and only confirmed product facts may be shown.
